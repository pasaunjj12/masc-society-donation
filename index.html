WHO WE ARE

More than a community. A movement.

Masc Society is dedicated to empowering masculine women by creating a space where they can connect, grow, and feel seen, supported, and celebrated.

Built Like Her extends that vision by celebrating feminine women, encouraging individuality, and creating opportunities for confidence, connection, and community.

Together, we are committed to uplifting the LGBTQIA+ community and making a positive impact through outreach, service, giving back, and meaningful opportunities that bring people together.
