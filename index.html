Our Mission

Masc Society is dedicated to empowering masculine women by creating a space where they can connect, grow, and feel seen, supported, and celebrated. Built Like Her extends that vision by creating a community where feminine women can embrace their individuality, confidence, and strength. Together, we are committed to uplifting the LGBTQIA+ community while making a positive impact in the community as a whole through outreach, service, giving back, and meaningful opportunities that bring people together. Our mission is to build stronger connections, support those in need, and create lasting change—one community at a time.

Community Impact

The impact we strive to make within our community is rooted in supporting growth, creating positive change, promoting equality, and encouraging community development.

Community Projects

As we grow, this space will highlight the projects, outreach efforts, events, and acts of service that allow us to give back and make a difference in our community.
