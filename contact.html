OUR COMMUNITY. OUR STANDARD.

Community Requirements

Masc Society / Built Like Her is committed to creating a supportive, inclusive, and empowering community where LGBTQ+ people can connect, grow, mentor one another, give back, and build meaningful relationships in a safe and respectful environment.

01

21+ Only

Members must be 21 years of age or older. We are building a mature community centered around personal growth, connection, and accountability.

02

Respect & Maturity

Treat everyone with dignity and respect. Be emotionally mature and able to communicate disagreements appropriately. Respect different identities, backgrounds, personalities, and experiences.

03

LGBTQ+ Inclusive

Masc Society / Built Like Her welcomes all members of the LGBTQ+ community. You do not have to identify as masculine, a stud, or a masc-presenting person to participate. Everyone deserves to feel welcomed and respected.

04

No Drama / No Toxicity

No bullying, harassment, discrimination, unnecessary conflict, gossip, or intentional drama. We believe in addressing issues with communication, accountability, and respect. Disrespectful or harmful behavior may result in removal from the community.

05

Willingness to Volunteer

Members should be willing to give back when opportunities arise. This may include community events, outreach, fundraisers, mentorship activities, or service projects.

06

Willing to Mentor & Support Others

Be willing to share knowledge, experiences, encouragement, and resources. We want members who are interested in helping others succeed, not just succeeding themselves.

07

Understanding & Compassionate

Be open-minded and willing to understand perspectives different from your own. Practice empathy and give people room to grow.

08

Motivated & Growth-Minded

Come with a desire to grow personally, professionally, and within the community. We value people who encourage others to chase their goals and become their best selves.

09

Can Work With All Kinds of People

You do not have to be best friends with everyone, but you must be able to coexist, collaborate, and communicate respectfully with different personalities and backgrounds.

10

Be a Positive Representation of the Community

Members represent Masc Society / Built Like Her when participating in community activities. Carry yourself with integrity, respect, and accountability.

THE STANDARD

We Aren't Looking for Perfect People.

We're looking for good people who are willing to grow.

Be mature. Be respectful. Be supportive. Be accountable. Give back. Lift others up.
