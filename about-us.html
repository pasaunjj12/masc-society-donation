THE FOUNDATION

A Vision Built on Belonging.

Founded by Kenya Mitchell, Masc Society and Built Like Her were created to build spaces where people can show up as themselves, form genuine connections, and know that they belong.

Masc Society centers masculine women, while Built Like Her creates space for feminine women. Each community has its own identity and experiences, yet both are connected by a shared purpose: empowerment, unity, and meaningful community impact.

What brings us together is the belief that everyone deserves a space where they can be seen, supported, respected, and encouraged to grow.

WHY WE EXIST

More Than an Organization. A Community With Purpose.

We believe representation, support, and community matter. Our roots are within the LGBTQIA+ community, but our commitment to making a difference reaches beyond it.

Through fellowship, community service, outreach, events, and giving back, we aim to bring people together and contribute to stronger communities for everyone.

Our purpose is not simply to create spaces for people to gather. It is to encourage genuine connection, inspire positive change, and help people recognize the value they bring to their communities.
