About Us

Masc Society and Built Like Her were created to build spaces where people can show up as themselves, form genuine connections, and know that they belong. Masc Society centers masculine women, while Built Like Her creates space for feminine women—two communities with different experiences, connected by a shared purpose

We believe representation, support, and community matter. While our roots are within the LGBTQIA+ community, our commitment to making a difference reaches beyond it. Through fellowship, community service, outreach, events, and giving back, we want to bring people together and contribute to stronger communities for everyone.

We are more than just a name and an organization. We are building a community rooted in authenticity, unity, empowerment, and impact!
