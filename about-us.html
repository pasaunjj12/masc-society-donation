THE FOUNDATION

A Vision Built on Belonging.

Founded by Kenya Mitchell, Masc Society grew from a vision to create welcoming spaces where people can connect around shared experiences while being respected as individuals.

Masc Society focuses on the experiences of masculine women. Built Like Her, its sister branch, celebrates feminine women. Each has a distinct identity, with room for members to connect through interests, experiences, and mutual encouragement.

At the heart of this work is a simple principle: people should be able to participate without hiding who they are or feeling that they must fit someone else's expectations.

WHY WE EXIST

More Than an Organization. A Community With Purpose.

Representation and belonging are important to a healthy community. While our roots include the LGBTQIA+ community, we welcome opportunities to build understanding and strengthen connections across the wider community.

We want to turn shared values into action through fellowship, service, outreach, and future events that create ways for people to participate and support one another.

We see community as something people build together. By making room for participation and collaboration, we hope to help individuals recognize the contributions they can make.
