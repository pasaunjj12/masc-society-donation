MORE THAN A NAME

There Is Power in Being Yourself.

Femininity has no single look or definition. Built Like Her makes room for women to express what it means to them—in their own way and on their own terms.

OUR PURPOSE

A Space to Be Seen. A Community to Belong.

Built Like Her gives feminine women a dedicated space to share experiences, explore interests, and shape a community that reflects their perspectives.

From personal style and creative interests to everyday conversations, we want members to feel free to define femininity for themselves rather than follow someone else's expectations.

As the branch grows, member feedback will help guide future gatherings, shared activities, and projects that reflect the interests of the women involved.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Rooted in Who We Are

These ideas guide the way we want Built Like Her to feel and function for its members.

01

Confidence

Making room for women to show up confidently in their own choices, interests, and personal style.

02

Individuality

Recognizing that femininity can look different from one person to another, with no single standard to meet.

03

Connection

Making space for conversations and shared experiences that help members get to know one another naturally.

04

Community

Finding ways to bring members together around shared interests and, when opportunities arise, contribute to local efforts.
