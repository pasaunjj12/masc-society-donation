OUR PURPOSE

A Space to Be Seen. A Community to Belong.

Built Like Her was created to extend the vision of Masc Society by creating a community where feminine women can embrace their individuality, confidence, and strength.

We believe femininity looks different on every woman. Our purpose is to encourage self-expression, meaningful relationships, mutual support, and opportunities to grow alongside others.

As part of Masc Society, Built Like Her shares a commitment to unity, inclusion, service, and making a positive difference in the wider community.
